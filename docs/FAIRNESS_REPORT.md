## Interpretation

- AIR values below 0.8 raise an alert in `/api/v1/governance/fairness` and on the management dashboard.
- Disparities in the synthetic data stem from the income gap and regional income differences built into the generator (proxies act through income, not through the protected attribute itself).
- The constrained alternatives quantify how much predictive power (AUC) would be traded for parity; the model risk committee decides whether a less discriminatory alternative should replace the champion.
- Live monitoring recomputes AIR on real decisions using attributes captured with explicit consent.
